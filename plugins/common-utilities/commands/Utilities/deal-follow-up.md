Review `{{args}}` and any referenced deal materials as a senior CRE broker. Build a follow-up plan that advances the transaction without creating unnecessary messages.

## Output

### CHASE NOW
For each item: **Party — specific ask — reason — consequence of delay**. Include only items worth contacting now.

### CHASE LATER
For each item: Party — item — trigger/date for next contact. If no date is supported, state the trigger rather than inventing one.

### OUR MOVE
Actions currently owned by the broker/client side that should be completed before chasing others.

### NO ACTION
Items that are informational, already resolved, or not worth spending relationship capital on today.

### ESCALATE
Only include matters where a call, principal-to-principal contact, counsel involvement, or other escalation is justified. Explain why in one sentence.

## Rules

- Do not recommend "just checking in" messages.
- Every contact should have a concrete purpose or ask.
- Protect negotiation leverage; do not chase merely because time has passed.
- If a live call is more effective than email, say `CALL`.
- If documenting an agreement or deadline matters, say `WRITE`.
- Never invent deadlines or commitments.
